After a decade of building a reliable distributed data architecture, the natural progression for Yugabyte was to begin supporting AI agents. Yugabyte is now a Silver Member of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), the Linux Foundation's home for the open standards that agentic AI runs on. This blog provides background on the AAIF's work and shares why we joined the Foundation.
